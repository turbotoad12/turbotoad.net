Static site generation isn't new—but it's better than ever.

Here's our approach:

1. **Write posts in Markdown** — clean, version-controlled content
2. **Run a Python script** — generates a JSON index
3. **Load dynamically** — blog page fetches and renders posts
4. **No backend needed** — pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

This gives us:
- The speed of static sites
- The flexibility of dynamic content
- No database overhead
- Easy to deploy anywhere

The future is yesterday. Sometimes.
